Contact us

Getting started

Menudiz shows fixed-price lunch (menú del día) restaurants near you on a map. Sign in with Google or Apple, grant location access, and browse restaurants with their menu price, what's included, and opening hours.

Sign-in issues

If sign-in fails, make sure you're connected to the internet and try again. If the problem continues, email us with the device and iOS version you're using.

Deleting your account

To delete your account and associated data, email us at alssamit@gmail.com with your request and we'll remove it.
